Diploma EEE 1st Year Papers

Diploma EEE Papers puts the AP SBTET C-23 diploma EEE first year board papers on your phone, solved question by question. Each question first explains the idea in simple English, then gives a full-marks model answer with one point per mark, worked steps, circuits and diagrams. Free, no sign-in, and papers you opened work offline.

Features

  • Papers for all seven first year subjects, March/April and October/November sessions.
  • Every question exactly as printed, with its marks; jump to any question from the chips.
  • "Understand it first": the theory in short, simple sentences for students who find it hard.
  • "Exam answer": a model answer sized to the marks, with circuits, graphs and drawing constructions.
  • Papers you open stay on your phone for offline study. Text size, light and dark themes.
  • Optional notification when a new solved paper is published. Ads never appear inside an answer.
